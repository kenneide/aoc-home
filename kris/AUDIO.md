# Christmas music

The show performs a new toy-orchestra arrangement of **Jingle Bells** (James Lord Pierpont, 1857), using locally synthesised notes, bass, offbeat chords and sleigh bells. No third-party recording, sample, service or download is used.

Composition reference: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/10535 (public-domain score). Historical reference: https://blogs.loc.gov/music/2010/12/jingle-bells/.

`music.js` contains the melody and original accompaniment, at 120 BPM. The 32-beat chorus leads into a build and final chord at 22 seconds. `choreography.js` uses that same beat grid. Audio starts only from the reveal/replay interaction. Mute changes the master gain; skip and lock stop all scheduled notes. Reduced motion skips the musical performance.
