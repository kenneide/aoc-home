Elf face studio ✂

Choose a person and a photo, then line up their face inside the oval. Keep the eyes near the upper third and the chin inside the bottom edge.

Everything happens in this browser. Nothing is uploaded. This is an oval crop, not automatic background removal. A close-up, front-facing photo works best.

Keep your work

Export downloads photos.json. Move it to kris/private/photos.json, then run npm run build inside kris. Refresh the demo to see the faces. Rebuilds preserve your codes and draw.
